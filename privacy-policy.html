Privacy Policy

Hanuman Chalisa app · Effective October 13, 2026

Hanuman Chalisa ("the app") is created by Venkatesh Akkara. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

To remember your preferences, the app saves a few settings on your own device only:

  • your chosen language for the prayer text
  • your preferred text size
  • your sing-along speed and chosen recording

These settings never leave your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Audio, text, and images

All prayer text, audio recordings, and paintings are included inside the app. The app does not need an internet connection and does not download or upload any data.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking tools. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing of information

Because the app does not collect any information, there is nothing to sell, share, or transfer to third parties.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if new features are added in a future version, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact Venkatesh Akkara at venkatesh.aka@gmail.com.
